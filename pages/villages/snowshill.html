A SUMMARY

Snowshill is a secluded hilltop village overlooking the Severn Vale, prized for its unspoilt cottages and extraordinary manor house.

Perched above the Broadway valley, Snowshill offers some of the most dramatic views in the Cotswolds. The village itself is compact—a cluster of stone cottages, a medieval church, and lanes that seem untouched by time.

Snowshill Manor, owned by the National Trust, was the home of Charles Paget Wade, who amassed an eclectic collection of craftsmanship from around the world. The terraced gardens, designed by Wade himself, are among the finest in the region.

The Cotswold Way long-distance path passes close by, making Snowshill a natural stop for walkers. Nearby Broadway village and the tower on Broadway Hill are within easy reach.

This is a village for those who prefer solitude to crowds. Misty mornings on the hilltop, with only sheep on the distant slopes, define the Snowshill experience.

01

Snowshill Manor & Garden

A National Trust property housing Charles Wade's remarkable collection of musical instruments, costumes, and curiosities.

02

St Barnabas Church

A peaceful hilltop church with views across the Vale of Evesham and the Malvern Hills.

03

Cotswold Way

The long-distance trail passes near the village, offering superb ridge walks in both directions.

GETTING HERE

train

BY RAIL

Evesham station (7 miles) or Moreton-in-Marsh (6 miles). A car is the most practical way to reach the hilltop village.

directions_car

PARKING

Limited roadside parking in the village. Snowshill Manor has its own National Trust car park for visitors.
